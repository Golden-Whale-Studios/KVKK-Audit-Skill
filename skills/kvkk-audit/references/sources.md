# Official sources

Last verified: 2026-11-01

Every legal citation in `rules/*.md`, `checklist.md` and `severity.md` points to one of the
URLs below. Only official sources are used: the Personal Data Protection Authority
(Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu, `kvkk.gov.tr`), the legislation database (`mevzuat.gov.tr`),
the Official Gazette (`resmigazete.gov.tr`) and the Ministry of Trade (`ticaret.gov.tr`).
This catalog is not legal advice (hukuki görüş değildir). Large projects should have their own
counsel review audit reports.

How each source was checked is stated in the **Status** column:

- **opened** — the page or PDF was downloaded on the date above and the listed facts were read in it.
- **exists, not opened** — the URL was confirmed to exist (official search result or a link published
  on `kvkk.gov.tr`), but its content could not be fetched from the verification environment
  (`mevzuat.gov.tr` and `resmigazete.gov.tr` reset the connection). Nothing in the catalog relies on
  these URLs alone; details that only they could confirm are marked "verify with counsel".

Article numbers are cited as "KVKK md. N (Art. N)". The Turkish text of Law No. 6698 prevails over
the English translation.
